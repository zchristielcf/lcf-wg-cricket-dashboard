3. Progression opportunities

Pathways

Choose one answer for each question. Genuine external club partnerships can count where they provide a realistic pathway.

A. Opportunities to enter girls' cricket

What opportunities does the club provide for girls to start playing cricket at the youngest ages?

  • None: No early-age entry opportunities through Dynamos, U9s or U11s. This does not mean the club has no girls' cricket provision; it may have older girls' teams only.
  • Limited: One early-age entry opportunity is available, such as U11s without a preceding Dynamos or U9 offer, or Dynamos/U9s without a clear opportunity to progress into U11s.
  • Established: A connected early-age pathway is in place, with Dynamos feeding into U9 and/or U11 cricket.

B. Progression through junior girls' age groups

How many different junior girls' age groups does the club provide?

  • None: One or no junior age groups; no identifiable progression to another age group.
  • Limited: Two different junior age groups.
  • Established: Three or more different junior age groups.

Count distinct age groups, not teams. Two U11 teams count as one age group.

C. Junior girls' cricket → Women's cricket

What is the highest junior age group from which girls have a realistic pathway into Women's cricket?

  • None: U9/U11 provision only, with no established route into older junior cricket before Women's cricket.
  • Limited: A pathway reaches U13 level, but there is a significant age-group gap before Women's cricket.
  • Established: A pathway reaches U15, U17 or U19 level, providing a more natural transition into Women's cricket.

An established external pathway can count if it is a genuine arrangement.
