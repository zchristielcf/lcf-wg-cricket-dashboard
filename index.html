Club development radar

How to interpret the flags

Provision gap to explore

A club has recorded girls’ teams but no women’s team records, or vice versa. This does not prove the club has no provision outside this workbook.

Progression conversation

A club has All Stars/Dynamos participation recorded but no girls’ or women’s team records. Consider checking the pathway, age profile and local context.

Coverage opportunity

Girls’ age-group records are present in fewer age bands. This is a prompt to check recruitment, retention and local demand—not a performance score.
